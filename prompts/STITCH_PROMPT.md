# Make My Marriage — Landing Page: Single Stitch Prompt

Copy everything inside the box below (from "Design a responsive..." to the end) and
paste it into Stitch as one prompt. Then ask for the mobile version with the short
follow-up prompt at the bottom. Logo: upload a PNG export of `docs/brand/logo-mark.svg`
if Stitch accepts an image reference.

---

```text
Design a responsive marketing landing page (desktop 1440px first) for "Make My Marriage",
a web app where Indian families plan a wedding together in one shared workspace.

TONE: polished, modern, warm, elegant, premium, trustworthy, quietly celebratory.
It must look like a serious modern software product, NOT a traditional wedding-invite site.

COLOUR (use exactly):
- Primary burgundy #6D1F2F; deep burgundy #4A1220 for dark sections and the footer
- Ivory background #FBF7F1; sand #EFE7DA for alternating sections and card fills
- Graphite text #2B2326; muted text #6B5F63
- Muted gold #B08D57 / #C9A66B for tiny accents only (small dots, hairlines, eyebrow labels)
- No pink, no bright red, no yellow blocks. Use burgundy with restraint: buttons, key
  headings, a soft glow behind the hero, and the dark sections only.

TYPOGRAPHY: Playfair Display 600 for headlines, Manrope for UI and body. Large confident
headlines, generous line height, small uppercase gold eyebrow labels with wide letter-spacing.

STYLE: lots of whitespace, 12-column grid, 24px rounded cards, soft low-opacity shadows,
thin sand borders, subtle burgundy-to-ivory glow behind the hero. Calm and uncluttered.

STRICT DON'TS: no stock photos of people, no floral or mandala decoration, no paisley, no
confetti, no clip-art rings or hearts, no ornate borders, no script or calligraphy fonts.
Visuals are product UI mockups (cards, dashboards, phone screens) with realistic content.

CONTENT REALISM: events Roka, Mehendi, Haldi, Sangeet, Cocktail, Wedding, Reception; names
Akshay and Princi; guests like "Rajesh Sharma, up to 4"; INR formatting like ₹12,45,000;
venue Dehradun, Uttarakhand.

LOGO: burgundy rounded-square tile containing a lowercase "m" drawn as two ivory arches
sharing a middle pillar, thin inner arch lines, and a small gold sun above the join.
Wordmark "Make My Marriage" in Playfair Display 600, with tiny gold caps "THE WEDDING
WORKSPACE" beneath it (omit the tagline on mobile).

COPY RULES: no pricing, no "free" claims, no fake user counts, no testimonials, no star
ratings. Never mention budgeting, payments, vendor booking, WhatsApp automation, SMS, push
notifications, seating, rooms, travel, custom domains or AI. Say "expense tracking" and
"Share on WhatsApp" only.

COMING SOON TAG: unfinished features get a small pill "Coming soon" (sand fill, burgundy
text, 11px, uppercase).
- Live today: Events, Tasks, Guests, RSVP and invitation links, Family members and roles,
  Organiser photo gallery.
- Coming soon: Expense tracker, My Vendors and vendor discovery, Wedding website with
  themes, Livestream, Guest photo uploads, QR code for photos, Email invitations and reminders.

PAGE SECTIONS, in order:

1. Sticky header: logo at left; links Features, Events, For families, Guests, Privacy;
   "Sign in" text link and a burgundy pill button "Start planning".

2. Hero: eyebrow "THE WEDDING WORKSPACE". Headline in Playfair on two lines:
   "Your whole wedding, in one place." Subhead: "Bring family, events, guests, RSVPs,
   vendors, expenses and photos into one shared workspace, instead of WhatsApp groups and
   spreadsheets." Buttons: primary "Start planning", secondary outlined "See how it works".
   Quiet line below: "Built for Indian weddings. Guests never need an account."
   Right side: a polished dashboard mockup card "Akshay & Princi · 14 February 2027 ·
   Dehradun" with a big "42 Days to Go", stat chips (Events 6, Tasks 32/48, Guests 186,
   RSVPs 132) and an upcoming-events list. A phone mockup overlaps the lower-right corner
   showing an RSVP screen ("Will you be attending? Yes · 3 people"). Soft burgundy glow behind.

3. The chaos becomes one workspace: title "Wedding planning shouldn't live in twelve places."
   A row of muted grey tilted tiles (WhatsApp groups, Excel sheets, Notes, Phone calls,
   Google Docs, Photo folders) with an arrow to one clean burgundy "Make My Marriage" card.

4. Everything in one workspace (bento grid, 8 cards with line icons, one-line benefit and a
   tiny UI snippet each): Events, Tasks, Guests & RSVP, Family members, Photo gallery (live);
   Expense tracker, My Vendors & discovery, Wedding website (each with a Coming soon pill).

5. Built for the whole family: left copy "Everyone helps. Nobody gets lost." explaining Admin
   (full control, manages members) and Manager (helps with everything else). Right: members
   list UI with initials avatars, role pills and an "Invite member" button. Callout: "Simple
   enough for parents. Adding a guest or ticking off a task takes a few taps."

6. Many ceremonies, one plan: horizontal timeline Roka, Mehendi, Haldi, Sangeet, Cocktail,
   Wedding, Reception with date, time and venue per node. Below, a guest row "Rajesh Sharma"
   with chips showing his invited events (Cocktail, Wedding, Reception).

7. Guests never sign up (deep burgundy section): headline "One link. One tap. No account."
   Three steps: invitation link opens, RSVP with headcount (limit enforced, "up to 4"),
   organiser sees the response instantly. Two phone mockups: invitation page and RSVP form.
   A "Share on WhatsApp" button inside the organiser UI as a normal button.

8. Photos and memories: headline "Every photo, from every guest, in one private gallery."
   Masonry grid of abstract tonal placeholder tiles (sand and burgundy tints labelled
   Mehendi, Haldi, Sangeet, Wedding), NOT real photos. Tags: "Organiser gallery: live today";
   "Guest uploads and QR code" with a Coming soon pill. QR card mockup: "Share the Memories.
   Scan to upload and view photos from Akshay & Princi's wedding."

9. Wedding website (whole section tagged Coming soon): "A beautiful website, without the
   design work." Three restrained theme preview cards (Classic, Minimal, Modern) and a
   livestream strip with a video player placeholder.

10. Trust and privacy: four calm icon cards: "Private by default", "Your wedding's data
    stays yours", "Unguessable guest links", "Guests never need an account". No numbers or badges.

11. How it works: four numbered steps: Create your wedding; Invite your family; Add events
    and guests; Celebrate together.

12. FAQ accordion, 5 items: Do guests need an account? Can family members help plan? Can I
    invite guests to only some events? Is our data private? What's coming next?

13. Final CTA band (deep burgundy): "Start planning your wedding." with an ivory button.

14. Footer: logo, line "The wedding workspace for Indian families.", columns Product,
    Account (Sign in, Start planning), Legal (Privacy, Terms). Copyright line.

Alternate ivory and sand section backgrounds. Use deep burgundy only for sections 7, 13 and
the footer. Keep all text legible and every section consistent with the same grid and spacing.
```

## Follow-up prompt for the mobile version

Paste this after the desktop design is generated:

```text
Create the mobile version (390px wide) of this same landing page with the same palette,
type and content. Header: logo mark plus short wordmark at left, menu icon at right; the
menu opens a full-width sheet with the links and a full-width "Start planning" button.
Add a sticky bottom bar after the hero with a full-width burgundy "Start planning" button
(52px high). Stack the hero headline over three lines, with full-width primary and secondary
buttons, then the dashboard mockup with the phone RSVP overlapping its lower edge. Make all
multi-column sections single column with 16px gutters. Turn the bento grid into a vertical
list of cards, the timeline into a vertical timeline with a thin burgundy line, and stack
the phone mockups. FAQ items are full width with tap targets of at least 48px. All tap
targets at least 44px, body text at least 16px, no horizontal scrolling, no hover-only behaviour.
```

## Handy refinement prompts

- "Make the hero dashboard mockup crisper and more realistic with ₹ formatting. No photos of people."
- "Reduce burgundy on this section; use more ivory and sand."
- "Remove any floral, decorative or stock-photo elements and replace them with clean UI mockups."
- "Add a Coming soon pill to the Expense tracker, My Vendors, Wedding website and Livestream cards."
- "Tighten vertical spacing between sections by about 20%."
