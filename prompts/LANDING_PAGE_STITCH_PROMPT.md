# Make My Marriage — Landing Page: Stitch Prompts

How to use: create one Stitch project. Paste **Prompt 0** first (the design system),
then generate **Prompt 1** (desktop) and **Prompt 2** (mobile). Refine sections one at
a time using the **Section refinements** at the bottom. Upload `docs/brand/logo-mark.svg`
(or a PNG export) as the logo reference.

---

## Prompt 0 — Design system (paste first)

Design a responsive marketing landing page for **Make My Marriage**, a web app where
Indian families plan a wedding together in one shared workspace. Tone: polished, modern,
warm, elegant, premium, trustworthy, quietly celebratory. It must look like a serious
modern software product, NOT a traditional wedding-invite site.

**Colour (use exactly):**
- Primary burgundy `#6D1F2F`; deep burgundy `#4A1220` for dark sections and footer
- Ivory background `#FBF7F1`; sand `#EFE7DA` for alternating sections and card fills
- Graphite text `#2B2326`; muted text `#6B5F63`
- Muted gold `#B08D57` / `#C9A66B`: tiny accents only (small dots, hairlines, eyebrow labels)
- No pink, no bright red, no yellow blocks, no gradients in pink/yellow/red. Burgundy
  is used with restraint: buttons, key headings, the hero glow, and the dark sections.

**Typography:** Playfair Display (600) for headlines, Manrope for UI and body.
Large, confident headlines; generous line height; small uppercase gold eyebrow labels
with wide letter-spacing.

**Style:** lots of whitespace, 12-column grid, 24px rounded cards, soft low-opacity
shadows, thin sand borders, subtle burgundy-to-ivory glow behind the hero. Calm,
uncluttered.

**Strict don'ts:** no stock photos of people, no floral or mandala decoration, no
paisley, no confetti, no clip-art rings or hearts, no ornate borders, no scripts or
calligraphy fonts. Visuals are **product UI mockups** (cards, dashboards, phone
screens) built from realistic content.

**Content realism (Indian context):** events Roka, Mehendi, Haldi, Sangeet, Cocktail,
Wedding, Reception; names like Akshay and Princi; guests like "Rajesh Sharma · up to
4"; INR formatting such as ₹12,45,000; a venue like "Dehradun, Uttarakhand".

**Logo:** use the supplied mark: a burgundy rounded-square tile containing a lowercase
"m" drawn as two ivory arches sharing a middle pillar, thin inner arch lines, and a
small gold sun/circle above the join. Wordmark "Make My Marriage" in Playfair
Display 600, with the tiny gold caps tagline "THE WEDDING WORKSPACE" beneath it
(header uses the mark plus wordmark; omit the tagline on mobile).

**Truthfulness rules for copy:** no pricing, no "free" claims, no fake user counts,
no testimonials, no star ratings. Do not mention budgeting, payments, vendor booking,
WhatsApp automation, SMS, push notifications, seating, rooms, travel, custom domains,
or AI. Say "expense tracking" and "Share on WhatsApp" only.

**Coming soon tag:** features not yet live carry a small pill labelled "Coming soon"
(sand fill, burgundy text, 11px, uppercase). Live today: Events, Tasks, Guests, RSVP
and invitations, Family members and roles, Photo gallery for organisers.
Coming soon: Expense tracker, My Vendors, Vendor discovery, Wedding website with
themes, Livestream, Guest photo uploads, QR code for photos, Email invitations
and reminders.

## Prompt 1 — Desktop (1440px wide)

Using the design system above, design the full desktop landing page with these
sections, in order:

1. **Sticky header:** logo (mark + wordmark) at left; links Features, Events, For
   families, Guests, Privacy; "Sign in" text link and a burgundy pill button
   "Start planning".

2. **Hero:** eyebrow "THE WEDDING WORKSPACE". Headline in Playfair, two lines:
   **"Your whole wedding, in one place."** Subhead: "Bring family, events, guests,
   RSVPs, vendors, expenses and photos into one shared workspace, instead of WhatsApp
   groups and spreadsheets." Buttons: primary "Start planning", secondary outlined
   "See how it works". Under the buttons, a quiet line: "Built for Indian weddings.
   Guests never need an account." Right side: a polished dashboard mockup card
   reading "Akshay & Princi · 14 February 2027 · Dehradun", a big "42 Days to Go",
   stat chips (Events 6, Tasks 32/48, Guests 186, RSVPs 132), and an "Upcoming events"
   list. A phone mockup overlaps the dashboard's lower-right corner showing an RSVP
   screen ("Will you be attending? Yes · 3 people"). Soft burgundy glow behind both.

3. **The chaos → one workspace:** section title "Wedding planning shouldn't live in
   twelve places." Left: a tidy row of muted "before" tiles: "WhatsApp groups",
   "Excel sheets", "Notes", "Phone calls", "Google Docs", "Photo folders", slightly
   tilted, grey and quiet. An arrow leads to one clean burgundy "Make My Marriage"
   card on the right.

4. **Everything in one workspace (bento grid):** 8 cards with small line icons:
   Events, Tasks, Guests & RSVP, Family members, Photo gallery (live today); Expense
   tracker, My Vendors & discovery, Wedding website (each with a "Coming soon" pill).
   Each card has a one-line benefit and a tiny UI snippet.

5. **Built for the whole family:** two-column. Left copy: "Everyone helps. Nobody
   gets lost." Roles explained simply: Admin (full control, manages members) and
   Manager (helps with everything else). Right: a members list UI with avatars as
   initials, role pills, and an "Invite member" button. Add a callout: "Simple enough
   for parents. Adding a guest or ticking off a task takes a few taps."

6. **Many ceremonies, one plan:** a horizontal timeline Roka → Mehendi → Haldi →
   Sangeet → Cocktail → Wedding → Reception as elegant nodes with date, time and
   venue. Below, a guest row "Rajesh Sharma" with small chips showing which
   events he is invited to (Cocktail, Wedding, Reception).

7. **Guests never sign up (dark deep-burgundy section):** headline "One link. One
   tap. No account." Three-step visual: invitation link opens → RSVP with headcount
   (max enforced, "up to 4") → organiser sees the response instantly. Show two phone
   mockups: the invitation page and the RSVP form. A "Share on WhatsApp" button
   shown as a normal button inside the organiser UI.

8. **Photos and memories:** headline "Every photo, from every guest, in one private
   gallery." Show a masonry grid of **abstract tonal placeholder tiles** (soft sand
   and burgundy tints with album labels Mehendi, Haldi, Sangeet, Wedding), NOT real
   photos. Tags: "Organiser gallery: live today"; "Guest uploads and QR code: Coming
   soon" (pill). Include a QR card mockup: "Share the Memories. Scan to upload and
   view photos from Akshay & Princi's wedding."

9. **Your wedding website:** title "A beautiful website, without the design work."
   Three theme preview cards (Classic, Minimal, Modern) in restrained styles, a
   "Livestream" strip with a YouTube-style player placeholder. Whole section tagged
   "Coming soon".

10. **Trust and privacy:** four calm cards with icons: "Private by default", "Your
    wedding's data stays yours (each wedding is fully isolated)", "Unguessable guest
    links", "Guests never need an account". No numbers, no badges.

11. **How it works:** four numbered steps: Create your wedding; Invite your family;
    Add events and guests; Celebrate together.

12. **FAQ (accordion, 5 items):** Do guests need an account? Can family members
    help plan? Can I invite guests to only some events? Is our data private? What's
    coming next?

13. **Final CTA band (deep burgundy):** "Start planning your wedding." Primary
    ivory button "Start planning".

14. **Footer:** logo, short line "The wedding workspace for Indian families.",
    columns Product, Account (Sign in, Start planning), Legal (Privacy, Terms as
    placeholders). Copyright line.

Keep every section visually distinct but consistent. Alternate ivory and sand
backgrounds; use the deep burgundy only for sections 7 and 13 and the footer.

## Prompt 2 — Mobile (390px wide)

Create the mobile version of the same landing page. Same palette, type and content.

- Header: logo mark + short wordmark at left, a menu icon at right; the menu opens
  a full-width sheet with the links and a full-width "Start planning" button.
- Sticky bottom bar after the hero: a full-width burgundy "Start planning" button
  (min height 52px) with safe-area padding.
- Hero: headline stacked over 3 lines, subhead, full-width primary and secondary
  buttons; the dashboard mockup sits below, with the phone RSVP mockup overlapping
  its lower edge, centred.
- All multi-column sections become a single column with 16px side gutters. The bento
  grid becomes a vertical list of cards (or a 2-column grid for icon-only cards).
- Timeline becomes a vertical timeline with a thin burgundy line.
- Phone mockups stack vertically in "Guests never sign up".
- FAQ items are full-width, tap targets at least 48px.
- Tap targets ≥ 44px, body text ≥ 16px, no horizontal scrolling, no hover-only
  behaviour.

## Section refinements (use after the first generation)

- "Make the hero dashboard mockup more realistic and crisper. Keep all text legible
  and use ₹ formatting. Do not add photos of people."
- "Reduce burgundy usage on this section. More ivory and sand; burgundy only for the
  primary button and one accent."
- "Replace any floral, decorative or stock-photo elements with clean UI mockups."
- "Add a 'Coming soon' pill to the Expense tracker, My Vendors, Wedding website and
  Livestream cards."
- "Tighten vertical spacing between sections by about 20% and align all content to
  the 12-column grid."

## Quality checklist before accepting a design

- No pink, yellow or bright red areas; burgundy is restrained.
- No stock photos, florals or ornaments.
- No pricing, user counts, testimonials or ratings.
- Unbuilt features are tagged "Coming soon".
- Nothing out of V1 scope is promised (budgeting, payments, vendor booking, WhatsApp
  or SMS automation, seating, travel, custom domains, AI).
- Mobile has no horizontal scroll and a visible primary CTA.
